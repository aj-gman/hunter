## Summary
Set in [[CITIES/Rome/Rome\|modern-day Rome]], this campaign pits [[Core/Lore (Hunter)/Organizations/Cells/Our Hunters\|the Players]] against a well-established congregation of [[Blankbody\|vampires]]. With a focus on strategy, tactics and roleplay, the story revolves on the Players and their every move to take down a massive organization, step by step. 

**The Cuckoo.** Work under the [[Core/Lore (Hunter)/Organizations/Conspiracies/Hototogisu\|Hototogisu corporation]] to expand commercial operations into [[CITIES/Rome/Rome\|modern Rome]]. Ally and subjugate both mortals and undying. 

**The Eagle.** The [[Core/Lore (Hunter)/Organizations/Conspiracies/Malleus Maleficarum\|watchful gaze of the Vatican]] permits Hunters big and small to attain a large amount of information on [[Blankbody\|the Enemy]]. But the Eagle has been poisoned by the Snake. 

**The Snake.** Uncover a horrid hierarchy of vampires who've lodged themselves deep in the throat of life in Rome. From radical politics to organized crime to local government, everything has been infiltrated. 

**Tradecraft.** [[Core/Systems/HTV/Endowments/Setto\|Steal and barter]] as you employ every necessary skill to conduct a war in the shadows. 

*You're covert operatives behind enemy lines - being discovered is a death sentence.*
