#tactics  
Sneaking into monster lairs to find human hostages or to steal powerful tomes from vampiric scholars is risky work. A cell must sweep the perimeter and check each room methodically to ensure no dangerous surprises await them, accomplishing the task both swiftly and thoroughly.

Secondary actors secure access to every part of the area, forcing doors or picking locks, and uncover any hidden passages or concealed spaces where a monster might be lurking. The primary actor then performs a final sweep to confirm that the area is clear — and if it is not, to neutralize the threat by any means necessary or ensure the team escapes intact.

**Action:** Instant or instant and contested; see below.

### Dice Rolls

• **Primary:** Wits + Composure. If one or more foes lie in wait, each foe contests with Wits + Stealth + Potency.  
• **Secondary:**
- Strength + Athletics or Dexterity + Larceny (opening portals, 1/5)
- Wits + Investigation or Survival (spotting hiding places or traps, 0/5)

## Roll Results
### Success
If the area is clear, the characters confirm this and face no ambushes or traps for the remainder of the scene. The primary actor also gains a +2 equipment bonus to any rolls made to investigate the area.  
If the area is not clear, the characters avoid any traps, detect anyone hidden within, and gain +2 to Initiative, Speed, and Defense for the first turn of any ensuing action scene.
### Exceptional Success
As with a success, but the bonuses increase to +3.
### Failure and Dramatic Failure
The characters overlook something critical — a concealed trapdoor full of zombies, a monster hiding behind a curtain, a trap triggered during the sweep, or a prisoner mistaken for an enemy. All foes present gain a surprise attack or a comparable immediate advantage, such as seizing an allied Storyteller character as a hostage or escaping without the possibility of pursuit.  
- If a trap exists, the primary actor springs it and suffers its effects.  
- If a prisoner or ally is present, the primary actor strikes them, ignoring Defense.
