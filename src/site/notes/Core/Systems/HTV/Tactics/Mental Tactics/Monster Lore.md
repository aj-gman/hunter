#tactics 
Too often, the information hunters need to prevail against their foes is badly diluted by urban legends and pop culture or hidden in forbidden tomes that have not been opened since their authors perished centuries ago. Even monsters that loom large in the public imagination, such as vampires, rarely follow the rules everyone thinks they know, and a hunter who relies on television for his lore is usually a dead hunter.  

The secondary actors call in favors, consult with experts, locate hard-to-find resources, and pressure information brokers in the supernatural underground. Then, the primary actor hits the books and uncovers the key to stopping the threat. 
**Each roll represents one hour of work.**

**Requirements:** Secondary actors must each possess 2+ dots in a relevant Social Merit, such as Contacts or Compact Status. Characters must research a specific type of monster.  
**Action:** Extended; total required successes equal twice the monster’s Potency, plus a modifier between +0 and +5, chosen by the Storyteller based on the monster’s type and rarity.  

### Dice Rolls
• **Primary:** Intelligence + Occult  
• **Secondary:**  
- Presence or Manipulation + Socialize (calling in favors / consulting with experts, 0/3)  
- Resolve + Academics, Investigation, or Computer (tracking down rare resources, 1/3)  
- Strength or Presence + Intimidation or Streetwise (interrogation, 0/2)

## Roll Results
### Success
For each successful roll within the extended action, the hunters learn one weakness the creature type typically possesses, one common Dread Power or other capability it usually has, or one element of information about its origins and nature. This does not grant information about a specific monster unless it is the only one of its kind. The primary actor gains the Informed Condition regarding the monster type.
### Exceptional Success
As with a success, but the hunters learn one additional piece of information. They also learn whether a specific monster they are pursuing follows the conventional pattern; however, further investigation is required to determine how it diverges.
### Failure and Dramatic Failure
The information the hunters uncover is not only incorrect but will produce catastrophic consequences if acted upon.
