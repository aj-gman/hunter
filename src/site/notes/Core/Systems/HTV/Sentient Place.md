A specific type of #location  in [[Core/Systems/HTV/Hunter The Vigil\|Hunter The Vigil]] #htv.

Perhaps strangest of all are sentient places. Unlike nests or tainted places, sentient places are intelligent monsters that actively pursue agendas known only to themselves. A sentient place can think and reason. It feels. It hates. It may even communicate, move, or alter its dimensions to attack those within its reach. Hunters who encounter a sentient place often do not know what they are dealing with until it is too late.
